Attention Mechanisms

Attention
Over Time

What happens to attention when the sequence is a signal,
and the measurements arrive whenever they like.

Syllabus

Topics

Sections 01 to 05 carry attention over from sentences to signals. Sections 06 to 09 break it, by admitting that real measurements do not arrive on a grid. Sections 10 to 15 rebuild it as multi-time attention, where the queries and the keys are made of time itself.

Assumed throughout: scaled dot-product attention, self-attention, multi-head attention, masking, and positional encodings.
